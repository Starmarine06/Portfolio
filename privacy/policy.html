This Privacy Policy explains how Dev Nambiar ("we", "us", "our"), an independent developer and publisher based in Bahrain, collects, uses, and protects information across our website (devnambiar.in) and the games we publish.

1. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • devnambiar.in — this website, including its contact form.
  • Games published by Dev Nambiar — currently Chamber Draw. Chamber Draw has its own detailed policy and terms at the links below.
Chamber Draw — Privacy Policy
Chamber Draw — Terms of Service

2. What we collect

Where Information
Website contact form Your name, email address, and the contents of your message, sent to contact@devnambiar.in when you contact us.
Website server logs Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps, generated by the hosting provider.
Games (Chamber Draw, online play) Epic Account ID, Epic display name, friend / roster data, lobby and session metadata, and match state, processed through Epic Online Services.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or data brokers. Play of Chamber Draw is local by default; the only third-party service it connects to is Epic Online Services.

3. How we use your information

  • Reply to messages you send through the contact form.
  • Operate and maintain devnambiar.in.
  • Run online multiplayer in our games, including lobbies, friends, and peer-to-peer sessions via Epic Online Services.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only disclosures are:

  • Service providers — our website hosting provider and Resend (which delivers contact-form messages to us).
  • Epic Games, Inc. — for online play in Chamber Draw (identity, lobbies, P2P, friends), per their own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Other players in a match — display name, seat color, and game actions.
  • Authorities / legal — only where required by law and only to the extent permitted.

5. Cookies & local data

  • The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Local storage may hold site preferences on your device.
  • Chamber Draw stores settings and optional Epic sign-in credentials locally on your device (User://), never on our servers.

6. Children

You must be 13 or older, or meet the minimum age required by Epic Games in your region, to play our games online. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the Epic Games account system requires. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we will work with Epic to remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR / CCPA / etc.) you may have rights including:

  • Access / Portability — ask what we hold.
  • Correction — fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to remove data we hold.
  • Objection / restriction — where processing is optional.
  • Most game data lives in your Epic account and is managed through your Epic Games account.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our site and games evolve. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date.
