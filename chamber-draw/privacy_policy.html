This Privacy Policy explains how Chamber Draw collects, uses, and protects information when you play the game. We keep it simple: play is local by default, and the only online service we connect to is Epic Online Services (EOS), which powers multiplayer, friend lists, and player identity.

1. What we collect

Chamber Draw is a Godot Engine game that runs almost entirely on your device. We (the developer) do not run our own servers and do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising.

Category Details
Epic Account identifier & display name When you play online, your Epic Games display name and Epic Account ID are used to identify you in lobbies and games.
Friend / roster data Which friends join your lobbies, their display names and chosen seat colors.
Game preferences Your chosen seat color, number of players, game mode, and settings. These are saved locally on your device.
Match telemetry Host machines track the state of an online match (turns, cards, bombs diffused, winners). This is game state used to run the match and is not used for marketing.
Authentication tokens / credentials On your device, a config file stores login method and (optionally) Epic auth credentials so you can sign in again. Never on a server we control.
You can play without an account. Offline mode against AI never touches the internet. Online play always requires a free Epic Games account.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage online lobbies and EOS peer-to-peer (P2P) sessions.
  • Route game intents (draw / play / diffuse) between players so everyone sees the same match.
  • Provide leaderboard / stats and friend features exposed by Epic Online Services.
  • Keep the game working — diagnose connectivity, update matchmaking, and operate support.

3. Epic Online Services

Online multiplayer is provided by Epic Games, Inc. via the Epic Online Services SDK embedded in the game. By playing online you are also subject to Epic Games' policies:

  • Data sent to Epic is handled per Epic's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • We rely on Epic for identity, lobbies, P2P transport, friends, and matchmaking. We do not host these servers ourselves.
  • Host-authoritative matches place game authority on the host player's machine.

4. Data that stays on your device

  • Seat color, player count, game mode — saved in user:// settings.
  • Match snapshots and card data — opponent hands are presented as card backs; their contents are never disclosed to other players.

5. Sharing (we share almost nothing)

We do not share your data with advertisers, analytics vendors, or data brokers. The only disclosures are:

  • Epic Games, Inc. — necessary for EOS login, lobbies, P2P, and friends.
  • Other players in a match — your display name, seat color, and game actions.
  • Authorities / legal — only where required by law and only to the extent permitted.

6. Children's privacy

Chamber Draw is generally intended for a general audience; we rely on Epic Games' age gating and their parental-consent handling for minor accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the Epic Games account system requires. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us via the game, contact us and we will work with Epic to remove it.

7. Security

  • Authenticated sessions use credentials stored in User:// (app-local user data), not plaintext on our servers.
  • Matches are host-authoritative and validated before committing moves.
  • We never store payment information — the game has no purchases.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights (GDPR / CCPA / etc.) including:

  • Access / Portability — ask what we hold.
  • Correction — fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to remove data we hold (most data lives in your Epic account, deleted through Epic).
  • Objection / restriction — where processing is optional.
  • Because we host no servers, most requests route to your Epic Games account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date above and, if significant, a notice in-game.
