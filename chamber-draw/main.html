Chamber Draw

A 2-7 player shedding-race card game you host yourself

Match cards by color or number, stack forced draws, steer the chamber, and be the first to shed your whole hand. Play offline against AI, or host a peer-to-peer match online with friends through Epic Online Services.

What makes Chamber Draw

Shedding race

Numbers and colors match like a fast, less predictable Uno-style race. First to empty their hand wins the round — then the table rotates.

Cards with teeth

  • Skip / Reverse / Draw Two / Draw Four / Draw Ten
  • Chamber bombs — defuse with a Diffuse card or lose a life
  • Extra Life, swap hands, peek the deck, rotate decks, jump in

Forced-draw stacking

Stack any Draw card onto a pending forced draw — even across colors — to keep the pile growing until someone finally draws.

Host it yourself

Lobbies are peer-to-peer and host-authoritative through Epic Online Services. No dedicated servers, no accounts beyond your free Epic account for online play.

How to play

  1. Set up a table. 2-7 players, seat colors, host vs AI or friends online.
  2. Match and shed. Play a card matching the active color or number, or an action card like Skip or Draw Two.
  3. Wield the chamber. A bomb slips into the deck. Draw it and you must diffuse it or take a hit — claim extra lives to survive.
  4. Stack the pressure. When someone is forced to draw, throw on any Draw card to increase the count.
  5. Win the race. Shed your final card to take the round. Keep playing while bombs remain across the table.
